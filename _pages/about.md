I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the group of Prof. Marco Garavelli at the University of Bologna, Italy. My research focuses on photochemistry, quantum dynamics and electronic-structure theory, with experience in wavepacket propagation (MCTDH, ML-MCTDH), surface hopping (TSH) and electronic-structure methods, including multireference approaches such as CASSCF and CASPT2.

I obtained my PhD in Chemical Science and Technology at the Universidad de Salamanca in October 2026, under the supervision of Dr. Susana Gómez Carrasco (USAL) and Dr. Cristina Sanz-Sanz (UAM). My thesis addressed the reactivity of molecular oxygen with organic and biological substrates, focusing on how intersystem crossing (ISC) and spin–orbit coupling enable and control oxygen-dependent photochemistry in enzymatic and solvated environments. This work combines high-level electronic-structure methods such as CASSCF, MRCI and CASPT2 to compute potential energy surfaces, minimum energy crossing paths and spin–orbit couplings, with quantum nuclear dynamics using MCTDH wavepacket propagation as implemented in QUANTICS.

## Other Research Lines

My research also covers nonadiabatic excited-state dynamics, vibronic-coupling models and the computation of kinetic energy operators. I investigate nonadiabatic excited-state dynamics of nitrogen-containing polycyclic aromatic systems using mixed quantum-classical dynamics (SHARC) and wavepacket propagations with MCTDH and ML-MCTDH (QUANTICS). I have also gained experience in fragment diabatization and in the construction of linear vibronic coupling (LVC) Hamiltonians for molecular aggregates, applied to unravel solid-state luminescence-enhancement mechanisms.
